Permanently deletes every student submission from the response sheet. Do this after grades are finalized to stop retaining student answers. This cannot be undone.

Clear grades in this browser

Grades are saved only in this browser (via the CSV export, not the response sheet). This clears them from here.

Removing grading entirely

To remove the grading feature from the site altogether once you're done with it, delete the "Grading" tab button and its two <section> blocks from instructor.html, and remove renderGradingRoster() / renderGradingPanel() calls from the script — then redeploy.
